How it works

Six steps. Most of them once.

What you do on the left, what AppRelease does on the right. No build machine of yours, no Xcode, no certificates to manage.

Once, for your account

1

Create your account

You

Name, email, password. A six-digit code arrives by email; you type it in.

AppRelease

Opens your workspace and shows a short checklist of what a release needs.

2

Add your signing keys

You

Upload the Android keystore, the Google Play service account and the App Store Connect key. Each form shows exactly where to get it.

AppRelease

Encrypts them, checks them with Google and Apple on request, and tells you in plain words if a step is still missing.

Once, per app

3

Connect the repository

You

The Git address, the branch, the package name and bundle identifier the stores already know.

AppRelease

Generates the one configuration file your project needs. You commit it, next to your code.

4

Connect GitHub

You

Paste one address and one secret into your repository's webhook settings. Two minutes.

AppRelease

Hears about every push from now on. Which branch and which tracks is your choice.

Every release

5

Push

You

Push to your branch. Nothing else. The version is read from your project.

AppRelease

Checks out the code, asks the store for the next build number, builds, signs, uploads to Google Play internal testing and TestFlight, and streams the log while it runs.

6

Tag to go live

You

Tag a version, like v1.4.0, when the testers are happy.

AppRelease

Releases to Google Play production and submits to App Store review. You get an email when it is done.

What we never ask for

Your Google or Apple passwords. Store listings, screenshots or pricing: those stay in the store consoles. Apple certificates and provisioning profiles: the runner fetches them with the key you added.
