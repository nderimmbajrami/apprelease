Legal

Terms of service

These terms apply when you create an AppRelease account and use it to release your apps. They are written to be read.

What AppRelease does

You connect a Flutter project and give us the keys to sign it and the access to publish it. When you start a release, or push to a branch with a push rule, our build machine checks out your code, builds it, signs it with your keys and uploads it to Google Play or App Store Connect, on the track you chose.

Your apps stay yours

You keep every right in your code, your apps and your store listings. You give us permission to build and upload them only as far as your releases require. You remain the publisher of record with Google and Apple, and your developer agreements with them still apply to you.

What we ask of you

  • Only connect code you have the right to build and publish.
  • Only give us keys and access that are yours, or that you are allowed to use.
  • Keep your own backup of every signing key. We protect them, but losing the only copy of an upload key is a problem only Google can fix.
  • Use AppRelease to build and publish the apps in your account, nothing else. No mining, no load testing, no running unrelated jobs on our machines.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. Everyone who works on your apps can have an account in your team.

Releases, the queue and your monthly allowance

Releases wait in a queue and our build machines take them in order. Usually one starts within seconds, but at busy times or during maintenance it can wait. The console shows where your release stands.

Each plan includes a number of releases per month, listed on the pricing page. Releases that are waiting, building or published count toward it. Cancelled releases, releases replaced by a newer push, and failed releases do not. The count starts over on the first day of each month.

What we cannot promise

We work to keep AppRelease running at all times, but we cannot guarantee it. Google and Apple decide what their stores accept, how long review takes and when their systems are down. When they change their rules or interfaces, we adapt as quickly as we can. A store rejecting a build for its content is between you and the store.

Plans and payment

The free plan costs nothing. Paid plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly, at the prices on the pricing page. You can cancel at any time and keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for. We do not refund partial periods, except where the law requires it. Before a price changes, we tell you by email at least 30 days ahead.

Ending your account

You can delete your account from the console settings at any time. What that removes is described in the privacy policy. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms, such as one that builds code it has no right to or misuses our machines. Where we can, we warn you first and explain why.

As far as the law allows, AppRelease is not liable for indirect losses, such as lost revenue from a delayed release. Our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the twelve months before the claim. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow us to limit.

Changes to these terms

When these terms change in a way that matters to you, we tell you by email at least 30 days before the change applies. If you keep using AppRelease after that, the new terms apply.
