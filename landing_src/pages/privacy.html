Legal

Privacy policy

Short version: we store what is needed to build and publish your app, and nothing we could sell.

What we store

Account data — your name, email address and password hash, and the team you belong to.

App data — the repository address, the branch, the contents of apprelease.yaml and the build logs. Logs are kept for the period of your plan (7, 30 or 90 days) and deleted afterwards.

Credentials — keystores, App Store Connect API keys and Google Play service accounts are encrypted with a key that is not stored next to the data. They are decrypted only on the build machine, for the duration of one build.

Source code — checked out onto the build machine for one build and removed when it ends. We do not keep a copy.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, do not run advertising and do not use your code or logs to train anything.

Processors

Hosting and database in the EU; transactional email through a European mail provider; payment through Stripe. A full list is available on request.

Your rights

You can export or delete your account from the settings page at any time. Deleting the account deletes the team's apps, credentials and logs. Questions: privacy@apprelease.store.
