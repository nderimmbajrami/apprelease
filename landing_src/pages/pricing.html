Questions about billing

What counts as a release?

One workflow run that reaches the upload step. A build that fails before uploading does not count. Re-running a failed workflow is free.

Can I change plans later?

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately and are charged pro rata; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.

Do you offer invoices with VAT ID?

Yes. Enter your company details and VAT ID in the team settings and every invoice will carry them. EU reverse charge applies where it should.

What happens when I exceed the release limit?

Nothing breaks. The release still runs; we send a note and ask you to move up one plan before the next month.
